Account Deletion — Aphri

Delete Your Account In-App

The fastest way to delete your Aphri account:

  1. Open the Aphri app.
  2. Tap Profile at the bottom.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion.

Delete Your Account by Email

If you cannot access the app, email us at pearlcenter01@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my Aphri account". Include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your profile information (name, age, gender, bio, interests, location).
  • Your photos.
  • Your messages and chat history.
  • Your match history and compatibility responses.
  • Your custom questions.
  • Your subscription is cancelled — no further charges will be made.

What We Retain

We may retain limited data for a period of up to 90 days for the following purposes, in accordance with Google Play's data retention requirements:

  • Legal compliance: Records required by tax, financial, or other applicable laws.
  • Fraud prevention: Records necessary to prevent abuse or fraudulent re-registration.
  • Safety: Records related to reports, blocks, or safety incidents.

After the retention period, all remaining data is permanently deleted or anonymized.

Processing Time

Account deletion is processed within 30 days of your request. If you delete in-app, deletion begins immediately. If you request by email, deletion begins once we verify your identity.
