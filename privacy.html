Privacy Policy for Aphri

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 · Last Updated: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Aphri mobile application (the "App"), operated by Aphri ("we," "our," "us," or "Company"). This policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you use our dating and matchmaking services.

By creating an account or using Aphri, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Aphri is intended for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly provide services to minors.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: First name, last name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, and password.
  • Profile Information: Photos, biography, headline, interests, education, occupation, religion, lifestyle information, relationship preferences, and personality prompts.
  • Compatibility Answers: Responses to personality, values, and relationship questionnaires used for matchmaking.
  • Custom Questions: Up to three personalized questions you create for potential matches.
  • Communications: Messages, photos, and other content you send to matched users through the App.
  • Payment Information: Billing details processed through Chapa, our third-party payment processor.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: Features accessed, time spent, interactions with other users, and app performance data.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we collect coarse and precise location to suggest nearby matches and calculate distances.
  • Authentication Data: Firebase user identifiers used for secure account management.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Unique tokens to deliver notifications to your device.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Firebase (Google): When you sign in with Google, Firebase provides your email address, display name, and a unique user ID.
  • Chapa: Payment confirmations and transaction status.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create, maintain, and secure your account.
  • Match you with compatible users based on preferences and questionnaire responses.
  • Display your profile to other users (name, photos, bio, interests, age, and distance).
  • Enable messaging and communication between matched users.
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions through Chapa.
  • Send push notifications for matches, messages, and updates.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and violations of our terms.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Improve features, fix bugs, and analyze usage trends.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. How We Share Your Information

4.1 With Other Users

Your first name, photos, age, distance, bio, interests, and compatibility score are visible to other users. Your email address, phone number, and precise location are never shared with other users.

4.2 With Service Providers

  • Chapa — Payment processing. Chapa receives your payment information to complete transactions. Card details are processed directly by Chapa's PCI DSS-compliant systems and are never stored on our servers.
  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication and push notifications. Firebase Authentication stores a unique user ID, email address, and display name.
  • Render — Cloud infrastructure hosting for our backend services and database.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Aphri, our users, or the public.

4.4 Business Transfers

If Aphri is acquired or merged with another company, your information may be transferred. You will be notified before any such transfer takes effect.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law, safety obligations, or fraud prevention purposes. Any retained data will be handled in accordance with Google Play's data retention requirements.

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the App settings. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile, photos, and personal information are permanently deleted.
  • Your messages and match history are permanently deleted.
  • Your subscription is cancelled and no further charges are made.
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

You may also request account deletion by emailing us at the address in Section 11. For full details, see our Account Deletion page.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Secure password hashing using bcrypt.
  • Authentication tokens with expiration.
  • PCI DSS-compliant payment processing via Chapa.
  • Access controls limiting who can view personal data.

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your account and personal data.
  • Object to processing of your data for certain purposes.
  • Withdraw consent for location access or notifications at any time through device settings.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11.

9. Children's Privacy

Aphri is not intended for anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we discover that a user is under 18, we will:

  • Immediately terminate the account.
  • Delete all associated personal data.
  • Report the matter to relevant authorities if required.

If you believe a minor is using Aphri, contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Notify you through the App or via email.
  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
  • Require your consent for material changes where required by law.

Your continued use of Aphri after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
